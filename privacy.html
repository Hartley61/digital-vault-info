Digital Vault Privacy Notice

Publisher: Mark Riley
Last updated: 18 September 2026

Your vault and your choices

Digital Vault is designed as a user-controlled tool. You choose what to store, whether to attach documents, who to invite, and when to back up, archive, restore or delete your information. The app does not make those choices for you.

Storage and synchronisation use the Apple Account and Apple services configured on your device. If you invite someone to a vault, the invitation is private and limited to the person you explicitly choose. You are responsible for deciding whether information is suitable to share and for choosing people you trust.

Removing a participant stops their future access to the shared vault. It cannot delete information they may already have viewed, copied or exported.

Information Mark Riley handles

Mark Riley handles only the information needed to operate the activities he actually provides. This may include the name, email address and information you send when you contact support, and feedback you choose to provide through TestFlight while the app is being tested.

We use this information to respond to support requests, investigate reported faults, improve pre-release versions and meet applicable legal or accounting duties. We do not sell personal data or use vault content for advertising, profiling or independent decisions about you.

Service providers

Apple provides App Store, TestFlight, Apple Account and iCloud-related services that you choose to use. Apple's own privacy information and terms apply to those services.

Retention

We keep support and test-feedback information only for as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, investigate an issue, maintain release records or meet legal obligations. We then delete or anonymise it where appropriate.

Your rights and contact

You may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability for personal data Mark Riley controls. To make a request, or ask a privacy question, email Hartley_61@icloud.com.

You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. This does not affect any other legal rights you may have.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice if the app or our processing activities change. The current version will be available at this address.
