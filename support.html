Digital Vault Support

For help with Digital Vault, email Hartley_61@icloud.com.

What to include

  • Your device and platform: iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  • The Digital Vault version and build number.
  • What you were doing and what you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened, and whether it happens repeatedly.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the issue.

Please keep private information private

Do not include passwords, passcodes, complete bank or card details, copies of identity documents, or private vault content unless it is essential and you understand the risk of sending it by email.
